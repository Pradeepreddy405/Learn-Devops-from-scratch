#	Ansible Interview Preparation

	1. What is Ansible, and why is it used?
	2. Explain the control node and managed nodes.
	3. What is an inventory?
	4. What is an ad hoc command?
	5. What is a playbook?
	6. What is a module?
	7. What is the difference between command and shell?
	8. What is privilege escalation?

# Intermediate
	9. What is idempotency?
	10. What is the difference between a task and a handler?
	11. What is the difference between copy and template?
	12. How do you use variables and loops?
	13. What are facts?
	14. What is the purpose of Ansible Vault?
	15. What are roles?
	16. How do you handle failures?
	17. How do you debug an unreachable host?

# Advanced
	18. How does variable precedence work?
	19. How do you manage development, staging, and production inventories?
	20. How do you deploy an application across multiple servers?
	21. How do you perform rolling deployments?
	22. How do you integrate Ansible with Jenkins?
	23. How do you discover AWS instances dynamically?
	24. How do you manage secrets securely?
	25. How do you test and validate Ansible roles?
	26. What are collections and execution environments?
	27. How do you troubleshoot a playbook that works on one server but fails on another?
	28. How do you reduce the risk of configuration changes in production?
	For every question, prepare an explanation, a practical example, and a troubleshooting scenario.
